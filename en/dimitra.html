Dimitra community listening clubs — FAO, 2016-2026

National expert in facilitation and monitoring and evaluation of Dimitra community listening clubs in Mali.

Context and my role

Dimitra community listening clubs are a dialogue and collective action mechanism supported by FAO, working with local radio stations. From 2016 to 2026 I led the facilitation and monitoring and evaluation of the clubs in Mali: training of leaders and supervision staff, coaching of partner organisations' facilitators, impact follow-up.

Clubs cover 59 communes, 26 circles and 14 regions (all except Douentza, Dioïla, Taoudénit, Kidal and Koutiala).

I also represent Mali at around ten international workshops and seminars on the approach: south-south cooperation between Clubs, methodological alliance between Clubs and agropastoral field schools (CEAP), listening clubs and community engagement.

Focus: leaders training in Kayes and Kita, June 2026

Under the Resilient, Productive and Sustainable Landscapes Project (PRPD), implemented by the National Directorate of Agriculture (DNA) and FAO, I facilitated as national consultant the training of Dimitra Club leaders, with Mr Souleymane Coulibaly (DNA).
